# H10 state test recipe

A manual sequence that exercises the Polar H10 states the recorder handles, so
one session covers connection changes, contact, motion, ECG quality, stalls and
battery removal.

## Setup

- Phone with Taiga Sleep installed and Bluetooth on.
- The H10 is awake and known to the app.
- Wear the strap with the H10 snapped onto it.
- Open the app and start with the sensor connected.
- Record heart rate, ECG and movement: enable ECG and movement in the data
  settings so all three streams are on.
- Keep the phone near the strap.
- Wait a couple of seconds between steps unless a step says otherwise.

## Procedure

1. Open Taiga Sleep.
2. Turn Bluetooth off.
3. Turn Bluetooth on.
4. Connect the H10 and enable heart rate, ECG and movement.
5. Start recording.
6. Wiggle the badge on the strap.
7. Take the strap off your body, leaving the badge on the strap.
8. Put the strap back on your body.
9. Detach the badge from the strap.
10. Wiggle the detached badge.
11. Remove the battery from the badge.
12. Put the battery back in.
13. Take the strap off your body and attach the badge to the strap.
14. Put the strap on your body.
15. Turn Bluetooth off.
16. Turn Bluetooth on.
17. Stop the recording.

## Long off-body variant

To exercise the off-body delay as well, keep the strap off your body for at
least 65 seconds during step 7, then continue with step 8.
